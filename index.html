    About This Game

    Puff is a yellowhead jawfish with cheeks full of sand and no plans to stay locked up. Captured by the hermit crab mafia, he wakes in the Coral Ghetto: a prison slum stacked out of bottles, cans and plastic, where small fish work for crab guards and alley-boss fish hoard stolen sea trash.

    Split It Out is a 3D co-op action roguelite with a cute, cel-shaded low-poly look. Swim, fight and escape one chamber at a time, and see what the trash we throw away looks like to the ones still living down there.

    SPIT SAND

    Movement is pure swimming, with a dash to get out of trouble. Your main attack is a spit of sand, with a stronger charged spit on a second skill slot. Every chamber is a fight against crab guards and every cleared chamber moves you closer to the top of the fortress.

    SORT THE TRASH

    Guards drop trash bags. Haul them to the upgrade vending machine in your hideout and sort each piece into the right bin:

    • Plastic powers your speed skill
    • Glass powers reflect-attack
    • Metal powers armor and shield

    Deeper chambers bring messier trash mixed with sand. Sort carelessly and your skills stay weak. Sort perfectly and you can smash through Don Hermit's fortress.

    ESCAPE TOGETHER

    Nobody breaks out alone. One fish holds off the guards while another hauls trash, and the crew decides together who gets which upgrade. Split It Out can be played solo, but it is designed around online co-op.

    IN DEVELOPMENT

    The demo is planned as Main Menu, Lobby, Stages 1 to 3 and an Ending. These features are planned and not yet built:

    • Boss fights that drop upgrade items
    • A between-run economy and skill progression
    • Collectible cosmetics
    • Voice chat and emotes
    • First-person / third-person camera toggle
